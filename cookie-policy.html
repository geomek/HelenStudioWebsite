This Cookie Policy explains how Helen Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on (the “Site”).

Quick summary: We use essential cookies to make the Site work and optional cookies (like analytics) only if you choose to allow them. You can change your choice at any time.

1) What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, SDKs, and device identifiers. We use these technologies to provide the Site, improve performance, understand usage, and (if enabled) support marketing.

2) Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary (Essential) – required for the Site to function (security, page navigation, consent status).
  • Preferences – remember settings like language or region.
  • Analytics – help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it (e.g., page visits, clicks).
  • Marketing – used to measure campaigns and deliver relevant ads (only if enabled).

You can control cookies in several ways:
